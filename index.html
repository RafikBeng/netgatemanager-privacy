01About the app

NetGateManager is a utility app for Android that monitors, analyzes and manages ZTE 4G and 5G cellular routers (such as MC801A, MC888 and compatible models). It lets you manage cell locking, band selection, signal metrics, Wi-Fi clients, firewall configuration, SMS messages stored on the router SIM, Wi-Fi spectrum analysis and internet speed tests.

02Network communication

All router management happens directly between your Android device and your router's local gateway address (for example 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1) over your Wi-Fi or LAN connection.

  • No relay servers. Router settings, router credentials and tokens, IMEI, IMSI, SMS content and cellular traffic metrics are never routed through or stored on external servers.
  • Direct gateway API. Management requests (HTTP/HTTPS) go straight from your device to the router's local web interface.
  • Other connections. The only connections outside your local network are to the services in section 7, our feedback server in section 8 and the speed test servers in section 9. Every connection that carries user data is encrypted (HTTPS).

03Router SMS

NetGateManager can read, send and manage SMS messages stored on the SIM card inserted in your router, using the router's administrative API.

  • The app does not access your phone's own SMS inbox and does not request Android SMS permissions.
  • Messages are read directly from the router's memory and are never uploaded to any server.

05Local storage

Settings and credentials are saved on your device only.

  • Router credentials. If you choose "Remember password", authentication data is stored in encrypted on-device storage (Android Keystore).
  • App preferences. Language, theme, polling intervals and display settings are stored locally.
  • Your control. Erase everything at any time by logging out, clearing the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

06Purchases & promo codes

  • All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card numbers or payment details.
  • Promo codes. When you redeem a code, the app sends the code, a device identifier and the app version to our server (hosted on Google Firebase), so the code can be validated and used only once per device.

08Feedback & router reports

Both are tied only to a random feedback install ID made on your phone (for example fb_3f9c…). It is separate from every other identifier in the app, including the one used for promo codes and purchases, so feedback cannot be linked to who you are or what you bought. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling makes a new one. You can see and copy it in Settings → About → Feedback and reports.

09Speed test

When you run a speed test, the app transfers test data to and from public speed test servers (such as Cloudflare, OVH, Linode, Leaseweb and Tele2). Like any website, these servers see your public IP address. No personal data is sent to them.

To show your internet provider and approximate location (city and country) on the results screen, the app queries ipapi.co, fast.com or ipwhois.app over HTTPS. These services see your public IP address and return network details that are shown only to you. We do not receive or store them.

10Retention & deletion

  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Feedback, screenshots and router reports are kept in our database for as long as they help fix problems and improve router support, and are deleted when you ask (see below). Telegram notices stay in the developer's private chat until deleted there.
  • Daily rate-limit counters (keyed by install ID or a one-way hash of the IP address) are deleted after two days. Our server's request logs at Cloudflare are kept for 3 days.
  • Notification tokens and installation IDs are reset when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Promo code redemption records are kept as long as needed to prevent a code being reused.
  • Delete my reports. Open Settings → About → Delete my reports. It opens the feedback form with a deletion request and your feedback install ID filled in; tap Send. We then delete every feedback, screenshot and router report sent from that install ID, and its rate-limit counters. A router report that other users also sent stays in an anonymous form: your install ID is removed from it and its user count lowered, while its total occurrence count stays. You can also copy the install ID from the same screen and email it to us.
  • To request deletion of other data related to your device, email bengrafikdev@gmail.com. Because we don't know who you are, include your device model and roughly when you installed the app so we can find the right records.
