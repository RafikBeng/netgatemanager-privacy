Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Short version: NetGateManager connects only to your local router. We collect nothing. We share nothing. No ads, no tracking.

1. What is NetGateManager?

NetGateManager is an Android application that allows you to monitor and configure your ZTE 4G/5G router directly from your phone via your local Wi-Fi or LAN network.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage analytics or telemetry are gathered.
  • No crash reports are sent to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers are accessed.
  • No location data is collected or stored.

3. How the App Communicates

NetGateManager communicates exclusively with the router IP address you enter in the app settings (e.g., 192.168.0.1). All communication happens entirely within your local network (LAN). No data from your router or your session ever leaves your device or your local network.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your Android device using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage. It never leaves your device:

  • Your router's local IP address.
  • Your router's admin password (only if you enable "Remember Me").
  • Your chosen app theme and language preference.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Internet Permission

The app declares the INTERNET permission in order to make HTTP requests to your router's local IP address. This permission is required by Android for any network communication, including local network access. The app does not use this permission to connect to the internet or any external server.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is a technical network management tool. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Since we collect no data, changes are expected to be minimal.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the app's GitHub repository or contact the developer directly via GitHub.

NetGateManager is an open-source project. No company, no ads, no data.
